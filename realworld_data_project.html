🟡 Glucose is Top Predictor

Fasting glucose accounts for 87% of model importance. All patients with glucose ≥160 mg/dL had a markedly elevated risk score.

🟢 Early Detection Window

Health Conclusions

01

Glucose + HbA1c Together Are Diagnostic

Both markers must be evaluated together. High glucose with normal HbA1c may indicate acute stress response, not chronic diabetes.

02

Obesity Dramatically Elevates Risk

The jump from Overweight (27%) to Obese (53%) prevalence is the single largest risk factor transition in this dataset.

03

16% Pre-Diabetic — Intervention Opportunity

These patients should be prioritised for dietary counselling, exercise prescriptions, and quarterly glucose monitoring.

04

Model Achieves ~84% Estimated Accuracy

The logistic model using Glucose + HbA1c + BMI correctly classifies ~84% of cases based on the scoring threshold of 0.55.
